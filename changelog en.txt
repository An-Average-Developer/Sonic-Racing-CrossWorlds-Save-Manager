3.0.1
## Additions
New Mod Store tab: browse CrossWorlds mods on GameBanana (search, sorting and categories) and install them in one click (zip, 7z, rar), choosing the files when there are several
Mod Store: an info card for every mod with its pictures, description, credits and files; mods you already have show as Installed or Update available
Mod Manager: each mod shows what it replaces next to its name (→ Shadow, → Blaze · Voice...), including styles, guest characters and voice, text, music or UI mods
Mod Manager: an Uninstall button (the mod's folder goes to the Recycle Bin) and a Reinstall button on each mod, which opens it in the Mod Store to replace all its files with the ones you pick
Discord and Steam profile pictures in Reports and in the Backups account lists

## Redesigns
The navigation rail groups Backups (Create, Restore, Manage) and Mods (Mod Manager, Mod Store) into one entry each, with a bar to switch sections (Ctrl+Tab)

## Changes
Backups show readable names ("<account>'s backup" or the name you gave it) and their folders are named "<name> - <date> <time>"

## Bug Fixes
Reordering the mods no longer makes the list disappear: they slide to their new place while a "Reordering mods..." screen shows
Long button texts in other languages now shrink to fit instead of being cut off
