## Additions
New Save Editor tab: edit any account's app.sav with the game's own pictures, names and sounds (the account is backed up before every save)
Unlock or lock characters, machines, parts, colours, stickers, horns, auras, gadgets, honor titles, challenges and music, with Unlock all everywhere
Your garage and gadget presets: build machines, change their parts, colour, horn and aura, and fill the gadget plate (Season 2 special slot too)
Courses: Grand Prix results, Time Trial laps and Red Star Rings at every speed
Profile, currencies and statistics: level, tickets, Duel Medals, race totals and each character's races and wins
Play Missions: see the weekly missions and complete them
Nintendo Switch saves: open the app file of a JKSV backup and get a zip ready to restore on the console
Tickets Menu: Duel Medals (Freeze, Multiply, x500) and Max rings in races (offline)
